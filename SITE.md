# SITE VISION: Hybrid Athleticism

## 1. Concept
An elite, AI-powered training platform for hybrid athletes. The platform quantifies the extreme, blending strength training with cardiovascular conditioning through a high-precision digital interface.

## 2. Technical Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
- **Backend:** Supabase (Auth, DB, RLS)
- **AI:** Anthropic Claude (Programmatic calibration and coaching reviews)
- **Design:** The Arctic Observatory (High-precision + Atmospheric Depth)
